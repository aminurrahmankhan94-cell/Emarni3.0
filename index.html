        Safety & Privacy Notice

        Emarni chats are not end-to-end encrypted. To keep the community safe, authorised administrators may review conversations, reports and account details (name, Emarni ID and email).

        When you delete a message it disappears from the chat, but a copy is kept securely for 30 days for safety review and is then permanently removed.

        Never share passwords or other sensitive information in chats.
